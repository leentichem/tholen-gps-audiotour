STOP 08 — VENKELSTRAAT / FLASH MARKET / THE FORECAST

[VOICE — calm, admiring]
This is perhaps one of the most beautiful streets in modern Tholen.

Look around you calmly.

An old facade. A front door. A bicycle against a wall. A plant in front of a window. Someone passing behind the glass.

Tholen is not a museum.

People still live here.

And that is precisely why the historic city remains interesting.

We walk further to the Vlasmarkt. The name recalls a time when flax and other agricultural products played a role in the life of the region.

At the end is the hull of the De Wachting flour mill.

The mill was built in 1848. Think for a moment of a time without electricity.

When the wind shifted, the mill could work.

If the wind stopped, you had to wait.

Farmers brought their grain to the miller. The grain was ground and the flour could then find its way to the bakery.

Perhaps the name De Wachting is therefore more appropriate than it seems at first glance.

[PAUSE — 2 sec]

A miller could plan whatever he wanted.

But in the end he had to listen to the wind.

[TRANSITION]
Now walk into the park and immediately turn right, keep right, and after about 100 meters leave the park at the Tiny House.
