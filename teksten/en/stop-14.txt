STOP 14 — EenDRACHTWEG / OLD TRAM LINE

This is where the story of Tholen's connection to the mainland begins.

There had been a tram connection between Bergen op Zoom and Tholen since 1882, but the tram initially ended at the ferry. A ferry was needed to cross the Eendracht.

The new bridge over the Eendracht was opened on October 10, 1928. That same day the first tram entered the city. The Eendrachtsweg was constructed as a connection between the bridge and the city.

The tram service was discontinued in 1934 and replaced by buses. Yet you can still recognize traces of the old tram line in the road surface. The old guardhouse is also located at house number 3.

The city went from sailing to driving here.
