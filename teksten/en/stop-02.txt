STOP 02 — TALKING HOUSE

[VOICE — slightly lighter]
You are now at the Praathuisje.

A small building, but the place tells a big story.

The Water Gate used to stand here: an entrance to the fortified city. Tholen received city rights in 1366 and was then fortified with walls, gates and fortresses. Later, during the Eighty Years' War, the defense was expanded again into a much larger system of ramparts, moats and strongholds.

Try to imagine how different this place looked back then.

Not a quiet walking route, but a clear boundary. Inside lay the city. Outside was the land and the water. Anyone who wanted to enter the city had to go through a gate.

The current Praathuisje has a completely different function. According to the municipal walking route, older residents came together here to exchange the latest news.

Actually that's nice.

Centuries ago this was a place where you entered the city through a gate. Later it became a place where people met and exchanged stories.

[PAUSE — 2 sec]

And that is exactly what we are going to do now: collect Tholen's stories while walking.

[TRANSITION]
Walk from the Praathuisje into the old town and head towards Brugstraat.
