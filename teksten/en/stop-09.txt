STOP 09 — TINY HOUSE / OUDELANDSEPOORT

You are now at the Tiny House on the edge of the park.

From here enter the Oudelandsepoort. The medieval Sint-Andries Gate used to stand here. The gate has disappeared, but the old passage and the buildings still clearly show that you enter the historic city here.

Along the way, look at the houses in the Oudelandsepoort. There are monumental buildings on the left. Pay particular attention to the historic facades and details of these houses; they give the Oudelandsepoort a clearly recognizable and characteristic street scene. The different facades, window layouts and heights show how the street has grown over time. The houses are close to the street and together form a quiet, small-scale cityscape.

In the nineteenth century, parts of the old ramparts were developed as a walking area. Where once there were defenses and soldiers, there were trees, paths and houses.

Now walk further towards Molen De Hoop.
