STOP 07 — MARKET

[VOICE — lively]
This is the place where you can imagine the sounds of old Tholen.

Horse hooves. A cart over the stones. A blacksmith. Voices of people meeting each other.

Look at the double travalje, the old horseshoe stable. Here a horse could be secured while the farrier did his work.

And look at the blue stone in the pavement.

In the past, a red-hot iron rim was placed on it. That rim went around a wooden wagon wheel. Then water was poured over it. As it cooled, the iron tightened around the wood.

A small piece of technology, in the middle of the street.

The Roman Catholic church from 1900 is also located on the Markt. The interior is richly painted. The altar cloth was made by painter Huib Luns.

And then there is the kiosk.

Here it was not about trade or administration, but about music and meeting. Stand still for a moment. Have a chat. See who else was at the Market.

The city pump dates from about 1760 and is designed in Louis XV style. It was also made by stonemason Nicolaas Muts, the same maker we met at the town hall.

[PAUSE — 2 sec]

Craft. Believe. Music. Technology.

Everything came together here.

[TRANSITION]
Now walk in front of the Catholic church along the Venkelstraat.
