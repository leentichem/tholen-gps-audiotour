STOP 05 — HOOGSTRAAT

[VOICE — warm]
Look at the houses on this street.

Some facades are large and stately. Others are much simpler. Together they show how different life in Tholen could be.

At numbers 13 and 15 there is a large house from about 1790. The shape and decoration reveal the taste of a wealthy client.

And this house has another special memory.

In 1809, Louis Napoleon, King of Holland, spent the night here.

Imagine that for a moment: a royal carriage in this street, people looking outside, curiosity behind the windows. For one day, the ordinary Hoogstraat became something royal.

But then life went on as usual.

That's perhaps the best thing about historic houses. They are not only associated with big names. They were above all the backdrop to ordinary lives.

A shopkeeper who opened the shutters early. A craftsman who went to work. A family lived upstairs. Someone who walked to the neighbors in the evening.

[PAUSE — 2 sec]

So don't just look at the most beautiful house.

Look at the whole street.
