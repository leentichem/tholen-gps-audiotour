STOP 01 — THE PORT

[AMBIENCE – 12 sec]
Calm harbor water. Gentle wind. Seagulls at a distance. Let the environment speak for itself first.

[VOICE — quiet, warm]
Welcome to Tholen.

Don't absorb anything at first. Look at the water. Listen to the seagulls. To the soft sound of masts, lines and water against the quay.

Our walk starts here, at the harbour.

For Tholen, water has always been more than just a beautiful view. It determined where the city was located, how people traveled and what they lived on. Ships arrived here with fish, shellfish and goods. And outside the city were the places where oysters and mussels were farmed.

Oyster culture eventually disappeared from this area in the twentieth century. The severe winter of 1962 to 1963 caused enormous mortality among the oysters. Due to the long time needed to grow new oysters and changing conditions in the water, recovery was no longer profitable.

Mussel culture also changed dramatically. The Delta Works and the Oesterdam changed the water movement and the salinity in this part of the Oosterschelde.

Yet the relationship with the water continued.

The current harbor was built in 1826 in an old, salty moat, after floods in 1808 and 1825. The harbor you see now is itself a part of urban change.

[PAUSE — 2 sec]

Look around you one more time.

You see a quiet harbor. But beneath that calm surface lies a story of working, waiting, sailing, storming and adapting.

[TRANSITION]
Now walk towards the wooden Chat House. There we come to the place where the Water Gate used to be.

From here walk towards the Praathuisje. Keep the harbor on your right side.
