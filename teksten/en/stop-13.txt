STOP 13 — BUTTER MARKET

We have arrived at the Butter Market.

This place was built in 1623 to improve the appearance of this part of the city. Botermarkt 9 dates from about 1830 and used to be a mayor's house.

Look around you calmly. The Market here is not only a square, but also a part of daily city life that has changed over the centuries.

Now walk further towards Eendrachtsweg.
