STOP 11 — BIG CHURCH

[VOICE — a little more solemn]
There is the Grote Kerk of Tholen.

The lower parts of the tower date back to the fourteenth century. In the fifteenth century the tower was raised to approximately 49 meters.

The church itself grew into an impressive example of Brabant Gothic in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries.

But also look at the details later.

There are more than a hundred gravestones inside. Each stone is a reminder of someone who once lived here.

The pulpit dates from 1648.

And then the organ. It was built in 1832 by Luitje and Jacob van Dam. It was originally intended for a church in Leeuwarden and later came to Tholen.

Mummies were also found during restoration work between 1946 and 1959. These are housed in the north portal, which is not accessible to visitors.

[PAUSE — 2 sec]

So you are not alone here in a church.

You are standing in a place where for centuries people married, mourned, listened, prayed and said goodbye.

The history of a city is not just in what was built there.

Also in who is buried here.
