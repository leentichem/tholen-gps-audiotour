STOP 15 — BACK TO PORT

Our walk is almost complete.

Follow the Eendrachtsweg towards the Contre Escarpe and the harbour. There you end up back at the water where we started.

Look around you one more time. From harbor to fortress, from old street to tram road: Tholen has always adapted, but the past is still under your feet here.

Thank you for walking through Tholen.
